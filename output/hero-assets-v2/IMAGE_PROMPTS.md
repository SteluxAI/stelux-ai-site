## hills_desktop

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: transparent PNG parallax landscape layer for a premium enterprise AI website hero, desktop edition.
Generate ONE high-resolution panoramic image, preferably 3840 by 2048 pixels, landscape about 1.875:1. This is a production background asset ONLY, not a website mockup.
Scene: richly sculpted, softly rounded moss-covered rolling hills at the quiet end of dusk. Original composition inspired by sophisticated editorial landscape photography. Four clearly separated overlapping ridges: softly desaturated warm gray-olive far ridge, restrained olive mid-ridges, deep evergreen close hills. Large rounded dark hill on the right and a lower broad hill on the left, with a shallow open valley at the center. Fine believable grass and low heath texture following the curvature of the hills, beautiful subtle grazing side light from upper left, softly readable shadows. Restrained, natural, detailed and cinematic, no yellow-green lawns.
Composition: image top 43 percent completely empty and genuinely TRANSPARENT (alpha 0). Hills start at around 48 percent down at the left edge, 43 percent at the tallest right peak, and 56 percent in the center. Hills extend continuously beyond left, right and bottom boundaries. Bottom 10 percent nearly black green #10150f for blending. The upper silhouette should have fine natural short grass, with crisp clean antialiased alpha edges; NO broad edge blur, NO fringe, NO glow. All space above the actual ridgeline must be real transparency; no sky pixels, no fog pasted over transparency.
Lighting and palette: muted deep moss, lichen, gray olive and charcoal green; subtle warm taupe highlights, dusky low-key exposure but retain rich visible detail. Soft realistic atmosphere BETWEEN hill ridges, not a glowing horizon outline. Premium photographic / exceptionally realistic landscape render, no painterly smears, no plastic smoothness, no grain overlay.
Must contain: hills and natural ground vegetation only.
Must exclude: sky, clouds, sun, buildings, roads, fences, trees, large shrubs, mountains, water, people, dashboard, UI, borders, text, letters, logos, watermark. A genuine transparent PNG, never a checkerboard drawn into RGB, no colored background.

## foliage_desktop

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: transparent PNG foreground botanical layer for a premium dark enterprise website hero, desktop edition.
Generate ONE ultra-wide panoramic image, preferably 3840 by 1280 pixels, 3:1 landscape. This is a reusable isolated foreground image asset, not a website mockup.
Subject: fine detailed natural banks of low heather, tiny-leaved shrubs, delicate seedheads and small olive-green leaves, with restrained dusty burgundy and warm muted bronze tips. Premium realistic editorial botanical photography at quiet dusk, matching deep moss rolling hills. Leaves individually defined and varied in size, highly detailed but naturally soft in the deeper shadows. Soft grazing light from upper-left, dark forest-green and charcoal undergrowth, subtle bronze glints, no bright saturated colors.
CRITICAL silhouette / composition: a broad asymmetric shallow U opening. The left plant bank rises from the left crop edge at 25 percent of canvas height, slopes gradually downward toward the middle; the right bank starts near 18 percent height at the right edge and slopes down toward the middle. At center 40–60 percent of width, plants occupy ONLY the lowest 10–14 percent of image height. This center opening is important for the website dashboard. All of the upper area and the entire large U-shaped opening are genuinely TRANSPARENT with alpha 0. Plant banks continue beyond left and right edges and are joined by a very low strip of dark fine groundcover across the bottom. Bottom-most edge is fully opaque continuous near-black organic texture #0a0c09, not a cutout floating above empty ground.
Show low shrub canopies and fine spray-like branching, NOT a row of full height trees, NOT a uniform horizontal hedge, NOT big broccoli pom-pom shapes, NOT a symmetry mirror. Vary bank height and plant clusters naturally, use a refined density of very small leaves. Have absolutely clean natural alpha matting around fine leaves, no gray/white/black halo, no rectangular visible background.
No sky, no distant landscape, no solid background, no painted checkerboard, no text, no logos, no UI, no decorative frame, no watermark. Output real transparent RGBA PNG.
