How it plays

Tap. Hold. Groove.

Four arrows. Four moves. Hit them on the beat — on a phone tap them, on a computer use the arrow keys.

1

Tap

The closer to the beat, the better the judgement. Land it dead on for a PERFECT — and watch the whole body react.

2

Hold

A note with a tail is a HOLD: press when it lands, keep it held through the tail, and let go right when it ends — the release is judged too.

3

Groove

Keep a combo going to fire up the stage, and fill the meter for Cheek Beat FEVER. In All-Star, all four play together — and trade places on the drop.
